Privacy Policy

Cibra: Food Noise & GLP-1 Log · Last updated September 25, 2026

Cibra is a personal food and body journal. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your entries, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Portions you add: protein, water and fiber amounts with their labels and times.
  • Food noise check-ins, side effects you note and their strength, the days you mark as dose days.
  • Weight and body measurements.
  • Your medication name (if you chose one), targets, units and settings.

This information is stored only in Cibra's own storage on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone at the times shown in the app. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Cibra receives only the status of your subscription, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier and purchase status to validate subscriptions and measure how the paywall performs; it does not receive any of your journal entries.

Analytics and tracking

Cibra does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete Cibra from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Not medical advice

Cibra is a personal journal. It does not give medical or nutrition advice, does not recommend doses or targets and does not diagnose. The targets in the app are the ones you set, ideally with your care team.

Children

Cibra is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
