Cibra

Privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Cibra is a private journal for people on a GLP-1. The short version: what you write stays on your iPhone, and the only thing that leaves it is the status of your subscription.

What Cibra keeps, and where

WhatWhere it lives
Servings you tap (protein, water, fiber, with labels and times)On your iPhone only
Food noise ratings, side effects and their strength, dose daysOn your iPhone only
Weight and body measurements (waist, hips, chest, arm, thigh)On your iPhone only
The medication you pick (optional), your targets, units and settingsOn your iPhone only

There is no account and no server that holds your entries. Cibra does not read Apple Health, your photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera.

What does leave your iPhone

Cibra uses Adapty, a subscription service, to show the right paywall and confirm whether Cibra Pro is active. Adapty receives a random profile ID created by the app, your purchase status and history from the App Store, and the fact that a paywall was shown. It does not receive your journal entries, your name or your email. Cibra has no advertising SDKs, no tracking across other companies' apps and sites, and no other analytics.

Payments are handled by Apple. We see that a subscription is active, never your card or Apple ID details.

Notifications

If you switch reminders on, Cibra schedules local notifications on your phone: a water nudge in the afternoon and a short check-in in the evening. If you start a free trial, it can also remind you the day before the trial ends. These are created and delivered on the device; no push server is involved. Turn them off any time in Cibra or in iOS Settings.

Writing to us

Settings › Write to us opens your mail app with a draft that includes the app version, your iOS version and the Adapty profile ID, so we can find your subscription. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Your controls

  • Settings › Delete all data wipes every entry from the app.
  • Deleting Cibra removes what it stored on the phone.
  • To ask us to remove the anonymous subscription profile, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com with the profile ID from your support email.

Not medical advice

Cibra is a journal. Targets in the app are numbers you choose; it does not recommend doses or diagnose anything.

Children, changes, contact

Cibra is not meant for children under 13. If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date. Questions: netkaktakto@gmail.com.
