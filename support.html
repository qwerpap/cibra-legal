Support

Something not working, or a question about your plan? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com, or use Settings › Write to us inside Cibra so the version is filled in for you.

I bought Pro on another iPhone

Open the paywall or Settings in Cibra and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Cibra. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

My entries are gone on a new phone

Cibra has no account and no cloud copy, so entries live only on the iPhone where you made them. That is also why they stay private.

Reminders do not show up

Check that reminders are on inside Cibra, then iOS Settings › Notifications › Cibra. Focus modes can also silence them.

I want everything erased

Settings › Delete all data inside Cibra. Details are in the privacy policy.
